# Sessions

The 37 sessions of UN Inter-Agency Career Week 2026 covered in this archive, in chronological order. Each entry says what you can practically take away from the session and whether the page has full notes or is still a stub awaiting the recording. The agenda ran 40 sessions in total; three are not yet captured here.

> **On method.** Each session was processed from its recording transcript through a small set of AI agents I designed for the job: one summarises the session, another extracts the reusable frameworks, a third converts the internal notes into the public pages you see. Every agent runs from a written spec that encodes voice rules, length budgets, and editorial judgement. AI handles the heavy lifting; I review every output and own the edits. Sessions whose recording has not yet been released or processed appear as stubs, marked accordingly.
